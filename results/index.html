Learning vs knowing

Variability should help while structure is forming (training from scratch) and be suppressed once it has formed (deploying a trained model). The MODULATE dial is the neuromodulation analogue: high to learn, low to deploy. Prior art: dropout (Srivastava et al. 2014), weight-noise injection, noise-aware analog training (Joshi et al. 2020). The claim tested is that native MoS2 variability (2–6 %) sits at the regularisation optimum, together with the substrate results. It is not a claim that noise regularises in general.
